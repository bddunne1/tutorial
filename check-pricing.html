How to Check Pricing

Every customer has their own pricing. Before you enter an order for a house account, check that the prices on the PO match what that customer should pay. There are three ways to look a price up, depending on what you know about the item.

Methods 3 Systems Customer Price List, QuickBooks Used in Order Entry, stage 1

Which method should I use?

Pick what you have from the PO.

Customer price list

METHOD 1Fastest when a list exists

Use the customer price list

At least their part # or our part #

  1. In Shared Works, open the file named Customer Price List

  2. Find the customer and open their most recent pricing document

    It's most likely an Excel spreadsheet. Every customer's looks a little different. Here's Box Partners:

    Try it

    Remember the Box Partners PO in Order Entry? It ordered TWR101 at $9.14. Row 4 of this list shows TWR101 is our 10000III at $9.14. The price matches.

  3. Can't spot the item? Use Find & Select

METHOD 2In QuickBooks

Check purchase history

See what this customer paid the last time they bought the item.

At least our part #

  1. From the home screen, open Inventory in the top bar and click Item List

  2. Search for the item #

  3. Right-click the item and choose QuickReport: 12345III

  4. Click Customize Report

  5. Go to the Filters tab

  6. Select the Name filter and enter the company name, then click OK

  7. You'll see every time that company bought the item

  8. Double-click an invoice to open it

    Use the most recent invoice if you can.

  9. Find the item on the invoice and read the Price Each

METHOD 3Find the item first

Search the Item List by description

When the PO only describes the item, find our item # first, then use method 2 to get the price.

Only the item's description

  1. Open Inventory → Item List

    Same menu as method 2.

  2. Search using what the description tells you

    Our descriptions follow a pattern. Look for these identifiers:

    5/32Diameter SOLID BRAIDAssembly NYLMaterial WHITEColor 500 FTLength SPOOLPack

    Item 18445III, from the Nassco order in Order Entry.

    Some words are abbreviated, so you may need some trial and error. Abbreviations seen in our item descriptions:

    NYLNylon
    POLYPolypropylene
    PLYESTPolyester
    TWISTTwisted
    DBL BRAIDDouble braid
    HOLO BRAIDHollow braid
    Tip

    Start with one or two words, like SOLID BRAID or 5/32, then narrow down by color and length.

  3. Found a match? Double-check with someone

    Always

    Ask questions unless you are 1000% sure it's the correct item.

  4. Now that you have our item #, follow method 2 to get the price
