Order Entry

Customers send orders by phone, through their dedicated rep, or by email. Every one of them gets entered into QuickBooks as a Sales Order. This guide walks you through the whole process, from reading the customer's purchase order to handing the printed paperwork to a rep.

Stages 5 System QuickBooks Have ready POs, a red pen, a stapler

The process at a glance

Work through your stack of purchase orders one at a time through stages 1–4, then print them all together in stage 5.

  1. 1VerifyFind the key info on the PO and confirm items and prices.

STAGE 1Before you touch QuickBooks

Verify the purchase order

Start by finding everything you'll need on the customer's purchase order (PO). Once you're confident the items and prices are right, you can enter the order.

Find these seven things on the PO

  • iShipping and billing address
  • iiPurchase order number
  • iiiOur item #, quantity and listed price
  • ivFOB and shipping info
  • vBilling terms
  • viDue date (if there is one)
  • viiAny special instructions from the customer
Key rule

Always work in spools. Some customers list quantities in feet, some in number of spools. Prices may be per foot, per spool or per case. You want the # of spools and the price per spool, because that's what goes on the Sales Order.

  • QUANTITY AND UOM Units are EA (each spool).
  • SHIPPING INSTRUCTIONS Freight terms are PPD/ADD: prepay and add.
  • Due date is in the Receive By field: 7/31/2026.

What to notice

  • PO # PH-38299750.
  • QUANTITY (PER FT) 2,400 FT and PRICING (PER FT) $.114/FT. Both must be converted.
  • The description says each spool is a 50' length:
    2,400 ft ÷ 50 = 48 spools
    $.114 × 50 = $5.70 per spool
    48 × $5.70 = $273.60, matching their total.
  • Read the General Instructions: shipping method depends on the UPS ground charge.
  • McMaster-Carr has a $50 minimum order, not $200.

STAGE 2In QuickBooks

Enter the order

We'll follow the Nassco PO (Fig. 1) all the way through.

  1. Open Sales Orders from the QuickBooks home screen

  2. You'll land on a blank Sales Order

  3. Check the template

    The Template drop-down in the top right should say AAmstrand Sales Order.

    Watch out

    MSC orders have their own template! Switch to it for any MSC order.

  4. Search for the customer in Customer:Job

    For existing customers, picking the name fills in:

    • Terms
    • Due date
    • Rep
    • FOB
    • Ship Via
    • Name / Address
    Good to know

    Shipping locations are stored as a 9-letter code: the 6-letter customer code plus the first 3 letters of the town. JOHDEEWAT = JOHDEE (John Deere) + WAT (Waterloo).

  5. Pick the right Ship To location

    If the customer ships to several locations, use the Ship To drop-down to choose the one on your PO. Check that the address matches.

  6. Check FOB and Ship Via

    These are usually right by default. You'll typically only change them when a customer is shipping differently than usual. Use this table to check:

    FOBWho pays freightShip Via
    Manteno, ILWe pay the freight and charge the customer later. If the customer writes PREPAY AND ADD, they want us to pay and add it to their invoice.“Best Way”, unless the customer has a default carrier or names one
    Collect / 3rd PartyThe customer arranges and pays for their own freight.The carrier the customer named, or their default carrier if the PO doesn't say
    FFAWe pay shipping and do not charge the customer.“Best Way”, always
    Tip

    When in doubt, leave the shipping info as the default, or ask if it looks wrong. Be sure to ask questions!

  7. Double-check every auto-filled field

    Compare terms, due date, rep, FOB, Ship Via and both addresses against the PO.

  8. Enter the PO # in P.O. No.

    The format must match the format of the customer's previous orders. Not sure? See How to check PO format.

  9. Enter our item # in the Item column

    The description fills in automatically. Use it to double-check the item # against the PO.

  10. Enter the quantity in Ordered and the price in Rate

  11. Repeat for each line item

    Move on to the next line until every item on the PO is entered.

Does this order need special handling?

For many customers you're done entering. Check the order against these four cases:

The order is under the minimum order amount ($200, or $50 for some companies)Minimum order fee →
The order is being paid by credit cardCredit cards →
The order has special instructionsSpecial instructions →
The order includes customer part #sCustomer part #s →

None of these apply? Skip ahead to stage 4: Finish the order.

STAGE 3Only if needed

Special cases

Extra lines, notes and paperwork marks for orders that need them.

Minimum order fee

MINORD

When the order total is under $200. Some frequent customers (MSC, McMaster-Carr, Fastenal, Wesco) have a $50 minimum instead.

  1. In the Item column, enter MINORD.
  2. It fills in our minimum order charge of $25 automatically.

Credit card

CRECAR

When the customer pays by card. Card payments carry a convenience fee, which we charge back to the customer.

  1. Calculate the fee by hand: order total × 0.025 (2.5%).
  2. In the Item column, enter CRECAR, and put the fee in the Rate column.
  3. Write C/C in red at the top of the purchase order, and on the sales order after printing.

Cash in advance

CIA

When the terms for this order are CIA.

  1. Write CIA on both the purchase order and the sales order.

Customer part #s

When a company lists its own part numbers, either alongside ours or on their own.

  1. Type their part # on the Sales Order, right after the description.

Special instructions

Some examples you'll see:

  • Include their customer's PO # on our paperwork
  • Ship after, before or on a certain date
  • Package the product a certain way (in or out of boxes, wooden spools only, etc.)

Type the instructions in the empty space below the line items:

For a customer's PO #, type it below the Ship To address so it's visible when shipping:

Important

Special instructions must carry over to the printed sales order. Check for them when you print.

Paperwork marks

These handwritten marks tell everyone downstream what's special about an order. Write them on the PO when you enter it, then carry them over to the printed sales order.

C/C

Credit card

In red, top of the PO, and the SO after printing.

CIA

Cash in advance

On both the PO and the SO.

EC

Exceeded credit limit

Top of the SO (after printing) and the PO. QuickBooks warns you when you save.

27515

Top of every PO once it's entered.

STAGE 4Save it

Finish the order

  1. Write the Sales Order # at the top of the PO

    For the Nassco example, that's 27515.

  2. Do one final check, then click Save & New

    Save & New is in the bottom-right corner.

  3. If a dialog box pops up, read it carefully

    “Credit limit exceeded”

    Write EC at the top of the purchase order, and on the sales order after printing.

    “Change this information permanently?”

    This appears if you changed a customer's default info (Ship Via, FOB, Terms, etc.).

    Never

    Do not change a customer's defaults permanently without permission.

  4. Enter the next PO

    Save & New gives you a fresh Sales Order. Go back to stage 1 with your next PO. When every PO is entered, move on to printing.

STAGE 5Once all POs are entered

Print & hand off

  1. Find Print at the top of the screen

  2. Click the drop-down arrow and choose Batch

  3. Click OK on the list of sales orders you entered

  4. Click Print

  5. Carry over marks and instructions

    Copy any special instructions and marks (C/C, CIA, EC) from each PO onto its printed sales order.

  6. Match and staple

    Match each printed Sales Order to its Purchase Order and staple the PO to the back.

  7. Hand each Sales Order to its rep

    The rep checks it for mistakes. House accounts are checked by Julie. That's it, you're done!

How to check PO format

Look up a customer's past sales orders to see how their PO numbers were typed before.

  1. Open the Customers menu in the top bar

  2. Choose Customer Center

    Shortcut: Ctrl+J

  3. Search for the company in the search bar on the left

  4. Set Show to Sales Orders on the Transactions tab

  5. Read the P.O. Number column

Glossary

PO
Purchase Order: the order document the customer sends us.
SO
Sales Order: what we create in QuickBooks from the PO.
FOB
Sets who pays freight: Manteno, IL; Collect/3rd Party; or FFA.
PPD/ADD
Prepay and add: we pay the freight and add it to the invoice.
UOM
Unit of measure: SP and EA (each spool), FT (per foot), CS (per case).

Credit card order. Written in red on the PO and SO.
