Pick & Pack

Use the Sales Order Fulfillment Worksheet to print a pick list and packing slip for an order. You'll work from the physical sales order, where the quantities to ship are circled, and do one order at a time.

Stages 4 System QuickBooks Have ready The sales order, a pen, paper clips

The process at a glance

Repeat stages 2–4 for each sales order you pick & pack.

  1. 1OpenCustomers → Sales Order Fulfillment Worksheet.
  2. 2ChooseFind the SO # and tick its box.
  3. 3Set quantitiesTo Fulfill Qty = the circled quantity.
  4. 4PrintRefresh First, Print, mark the SO, clear selections.
STAGE 1In QuickBooks

Open the fulfillment worksheet

  1. From the home screen, open Customers in the top bar and choose Sales Order Fulfillment Worksheet

  2. Make sure Sort (top right) is set to Customer:Job

STAGE 2One order at a time

Choose the order

In this example we're picking & packing SO# 27568 for McMaster-Carr.

  1. Scroll to the sales order # you want to pick

  2. Tick the box in the far-left Choose column

  3. The bottom half of the screen now shows the order's lines

    The circle next to each item shows what's in stock:

    Green: enough in stock for the line
    Yellow: some in stock, but not the full amount
    Red ✕: none available

  4. Go through each item and check the To Fulfill Qty and the Rate

STAGE 3The most important step

Set quantities from the circles

On the physical sales order, the quantity we want to ship is circled. That's the number that goes in To Fulfill Qty.

Rule

Always use the circled quantity on the physical sales order you're picking & packing.

If an item has no circled quantity, it is not shipping. Set its To Fulfill Qty to 0.

STAGE 4Once quantities are right

Print & paperwork

  1. Click Refresh First, then Print

  2. Write P & P at the top of the sales order, with the date and your initials

  3. Clear your selections after every print

    Use the Clear Choices button under the list of sales orders.

    Watch out

    If you don't clear your selections, the next print will include everything you've selected. We only print one order at a time.

  4. Paper-clip the printed pick list and packing slip together

    MSC orders need a different packing slip. See MSC packing slips.

  5. Lay them with the sales order

    That order is done. Go back to stage 2 for the next one.

REF AWhen something's different

Notes & exceptions

MSC packing slips

MSC Packing Slip

MSC pick & packs need a special packing slip.

  1. Open the sales order you just pick & packed.
  2. Change the Template (top right) to MSC Packing Slip.
  3. Print the sales order. That gives you the MSC packing slip.

Circled, but not enough in stock

An item has a circled quantity, but QuickBooks doesn't show enough stock for it.

  1. Check if it's a work order. Work-ordered items are made in house, and inventory may not be updated yet.
  2. If it isn't a work order, there may be a mistake. Check with Jillian that the circled quantity is correct.

Partial shipment with stock available

Sometimes we ship only part of a line even though there's enough in stock to ship it all.

That's not a mistake. Still enter the circled quantity.
